## 3. COMPREHENSIVE SPECIFICATION OF ASSIGNED CLAIMS
This grant of exclusive commercial operation rights specifically covers, assigns, and secures the following proprietary operational, mathematical, and algorithmic **Claims** to The Boeing Company:

### Clause 3.1: Mechanical Architecture Claims
* **The Distributed Core Matrix Claim:** The physical and structural distribution of exactly eighteen (18) forced-induction HEMI reciprocating internal combustion powerplants arranged as a distributed propulsion network, specifically deployed in a balanced matrix configuration consisting of exactly nine (9) coaxial core units per wing.
* **The Kinematic Power Transmission Claim:** The specific mechanical integration of an epicyclic (planetary) reduction gearbox matrix engineered at a fixed reduction ratio of **5.476:1**, transferring torque from the input HEMI crankshaft operating at high rotational velocities directly to a subsonic, variable-pitch coaxial propeller assembly.

### Clause 3.2: Thermodynamic & High-Altitude Performance Claims
* **The Isentropic Multi-Stage Compression Claim:** The proprietary thermodynamic scaling method utilizing a multi-stage forced induction pressure ratio of exactly **4.25:1**, mathematically calibrated to deliver sea-level equivalent air mass flow to internal displacement chambers within an ambient atmospheric density envelope of **0.3796 kg/m³** at a cruise altitude of 10,668 meters (35,000 feet).
* **The Thermal Ingestion Mitigation Claim:** The mathematical modeling and execution of the charge-air temperature differentials governed by the isentropic compressor efficiency loop, optimized for high-altitude thermodynamic survival down to ambient temperatures of 218.8 K.

### Clause 3.3: Software Control & Algorithmic Claims
* **The Concurrent ISO C++20 Multitasking Claim:** The embedded real-time software control architecture executing at a strict deterministic frequency of **100Hz**, driven by cooperative multi-threading routines utilizing `std::jthread` and lock-free atomic telemetry state exchanges (`std::atomic` under explicit memory order constraints).
* **The Dual-Governor Discrete PID Synchronization Claim:** The proprietary algorithmic implementation of dual-layered, independent Proportional-Integral-Derivative (PID) control matrices acting concurrently per core to dynamically synchronize rotational velocities (RPM) and variable-pitch aerodynamics.
* **The Automated Fail-Safe Propeller Feathering Claim:** The programmatic fault-injection and automated fail-safe recovery logic that isolates localized mechanical core anomalies and instantly forces propeller blade actuation to an aerodynamic neutral state of exactly **90.0 degrees (feathered position)** to eliminate asymmetric drag penalties.
